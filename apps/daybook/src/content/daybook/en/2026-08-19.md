## Court lets Ethiopia TPS termination proceed while a bias claim continues

**A federal judge declined to keep Temporary Protected Status (TPS) protections in place for Ethiopian nationals** while their legal challenge continues. The ruling allows the administration to move ahead with ending the designation, while plaintiffs may continue pursuing a claim that racial or national-origin bias influenced the decision. [Newsweek](https://newsweek.com/map-extent-trump-tps-terminations-which-countries-next-12338465), [Straits Times](https://straitstimes.com/world/us-judge-allows-trump-to-end-thousands-of-ethiopians-deportation-protections), [CNN en Español](https://cnnespanol.cnn.com/2026/08/18/eeuu/tps-etiope-deportacion-fin-protecciones-trax)

**Recent weeks have seen TPS protections expire for several groups.** People who lose TPS can also lose work authorization and protection from removal if they do not have another immigration status or avenue available. [Sahan Journal](https://sahanjournal.com/immigration/tps-cut-myanmar-minnesota-karen-community-trump-immigration)

## USCIS updates public-charge guidance for permanent-residence applicants

**U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) has published updated guidance on public-charge inadmissibility for covered applicants seeking adjustment to lawful permanent residence.** The guidance explains which immigration categories are exempt and how a public-charge bond may be considered. [USCIS](https://www.uscis.gov/newsroom/alerts/uscis-issues-guidance-on-making-public-charge-inadmissibility-determination), [Telemundo McAllen](https://telemundo40.com/noticias/local/uscis-amplia-criterios-de-carga-publica-para-quienes-solicitan-la-residencia-permanente/2540378), [El Nuevo Herald](https://elnuevoherald.com/noticias/acceso-miami/article316923223.html)

**Officers are directed to assess the totality of the circumstances rather than treat one benefit or personal factor as automatically decisive.** The factors identified include age, health, family circumstances, financial resources, education and skills, so the review turns on the applicant’s circumstances as a whole. [El Comercio](https://elcomercio.pe/mag/usa/local-us/uscis-cambia-las-reglas-de-carga-publica-estados-unidos-esto-tomara-en-cuenta-para-decidir-si-eres-inadmisible-nnda-nnni-noticia)

## Court oversight and new detention plans put immigrant children in focus

**A court has moved to require additional independent oversight connected to protections for children in immigration detention.** That puts compliance with safeguards for children held by the government under closer judicial attention. [The Marshall Project](https://themarshallproject.org/2026/08/18/parents-immigrant-camps-children-deportation-flores), [El Diario](https://eldiariony.com/2026/08/18/corte-federal-ordena-supervision-independiente-sobre-la-detencion-de-menores-migrantes)

**Federal officials are seeking operators for an emergency facility intended to hold unaccompanied immigrant children.** The proposed site would expand custody capacity and has drawn attention to the conditions and services available to children in government care. [The Independent](https://www.independent.co.uk/news/world/americas/us-politics/trump-migrant-children-detention-plans-b3035247.html)

**The Independent article appears to refer to a contract originally posted July 16** to the System for Award Management. [SAM.gov](https://sam.gov/opp/37ea753884174b2d88298f4f2505051e/view)

## Detainees describe medical-care and health concerns at Colorado ICE facility

**Former detainees, a health worker and records reviewed by Telemundo describe concerns about medical attention, respiratory and gastrointestinal illnesses, food quality and tuberculosis-related conditions** at Immigration and Customs Enforcement’s (ICE) processing center in Aurora, Colorado. ICE said there were no active tuberculosis cases or people still subject to contact tracing at the facility. [Telemundo](https://telemundo.com/noticias/noticias-telemundo/inmigracion/ice-aurora-problemas-salud-inmigrantes-detenidos-atencion-medica-rcna593171)

**Broader reporting examines a disconnect between ICE facility health ratings and reported health concerns, including deaths in detention.** The gap raises questions about what formal inspections and evaluations capture, making this a distinctive Spanish-language reporting lane in the day’s coverage. [Project on Government Oversight](https://pogo.org/investigates/inside-ices-broken-system-for-inspecting-detention-facilities), [CalMatters](https://calmatters.org/calmatters-en-espanol/2026/08/informe-ice-salud-a-centros-de-detencion), [KPBS Public Media](https://kpbs.org/news/informe-ice-da-altas-notas-de-salud-a-centros-de-detencion-pese-a-aumento-de-muertes)
