## North Carolina’s veto override is bringing state agencies into ICE’s 287(g) system

**North Carolina lawmakers overrode Gov. Josh Stein’s veto in June, requiring four state public-safety agencies to enter ICE’s 287(g) program.** The State Highway Patrol signed a Task Force Model agreement on September 27, days before an October 1 deadline tied to Senate Bill 153, the Border Protection Act. [Enlace Latino NC](https://enlacelatinonc.org/estos-son-los-acuerdos-287g-firmados-con-ice-por-tres-de-las-agencias-del-orden-de-carolina-del-norte/), [Conexión Migrante](https://conexionmigrante.com/2026-/09-/30/patrulla-de-carreteras-podra-colaborar-con-ice-durante-paradas-de-transito-en-carolina-del-norte/)

The law names the Department of Public Safety, Department of Adult Correction, State Highway Patrol and State Bureau of Investigation. The Herald-Sun reported that Adult Correction signed a Warrant Service Officer agreement and that the agencies had to submit their agreements and policies to legislative overseers by October 1. [The Herald-Sun](https://www.heraldsun.com/news/politics-government/article317446513.html) See the [North Carolina roster on 287(g) Watch](https://287g.recoveredfactory.net/en/state/nc).

## MTC will reopen an Arizona prison as an ICE jail

**Management and Training Corporation, the private prison company known as MTC, says it will reopen the former Marana prison in Arizona as an ICE detention center within 60 days.** MTC told Arizona Luminaria the facility is expected to hold 775 people and employ 338 full-time workers. [Arizona Luminaria](https://azluminaria.org/2026/09/30/mtc-says-it-will-open-ice-detention-center-in-marana-within-60-days/)

**The reopening returns ICE detention to a prison marked by unresolved reporting about its years under MTC.** LOOKOUT described a former employee’s account, corroborated in part by another former employee and state investigative records, that allegations of staff sexual misconduct were left out of a 2023 Prison Rape Elimination Act audit; MTC, the former warden and the Arizona Department of Corrections did not respond to the reporter’s requests for comment. [LOOKOUT](https://www.lookoutnews.org/mtc-facility-did-not-report-sexual-assault-reopens-as-ice-jail)

**I worked with Joe Neff and Alysia Santo on an investigation of MTC’s no-show prison workers in Mississippi.** Our analysis conservatively estimated that MTC received millions of dollars for work it did not perform. [The Marshall Project](https://www.themarshallproject.org/2022/11/15/mississippi-auditor-prison-company-must-pay-2-million-for-no-show-workers)

## A record year of deaths in ICE custody as watchdog capacity shrinks

**Thirty-seven people have died while held in ICE custody since last fall, Bloomberg Law reports—the agency’s deadliest fiscal year on record.** The deaths come as detention capacity expands and the administration has cut the DHS civil-rights office and eliminated funding for the Office of the Immigration Detention Ombudsman. [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/us-law-week/immigrant-deaths-in-ice-custody-nearly-double-as-oversight-wanes)

## Upcoming

### This week (Mon–Fri)

- **OCT 1:** A new fiscal year's supply of employment-based green card numbers becomes available on October 1, so the October 2026 Visa Bulletin — the State Department chart that sets who can file or be approved based on their place in line (priority date) — may show forward movement in backlogged categories such as EB-2 India. [WR Immigration (Wolfsdorf Rosenthal)](https://wolfsdorf.com/wr-immigration-news-digest-20260618/)
- **OCT 2:** USCIS says certain TPS work documents issued under Venezuela's 2023 designation remain valid through October 2 under a federal court order. We added this date because USCIS still identifies it, while its effect remains tied to litigation. [USCIS](https://www.uscis.gov/humanitarian/temporary-protected-status/temporary-protected-status-designated-country-venezuela)

### Next four weeks

- **OCT 5:** The federal office that houses immigrant children who arrive without a parent or guardian, the Office of Refugee Resettlement (ORR), wants to revise the forms its inspectors use to check whether shelters are meeting licensing rules and other legal requirements. Anyone can comment until October 5, 2026. [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/08/05/2026-15842/proposed-information-collection-activity-monitoring-and-compliance-for-office-of-refugee)
- **OCT 13:** USCIS can now require that certain applications and petitions be filed online rather than on paper, and the rule sets out how it will designate which ones. People who cannot file electronically can ask for a waiver. The change is already in force as an interim final rule, but comments are open until October 13, 2026. [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/08/11/2026-16313/mandatory-electronic-filing-e-filing)
- **OCT 19:** For Ukraine, USCIS lists October 19, 2026, on TPS documents; certain Sudan TPS work documents also remain valid through October 19, 2026. This is a date to watch, not a settled end date: Ukraine's statutory extension question and official implementation remain unresolved. [USCIS](https://www.uscis.gov/humanitarian/temporary-protected-status/TPS-Ukraine)
