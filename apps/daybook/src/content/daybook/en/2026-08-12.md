## As TPS ends, consequences ripple across multiple communities

**Losing Temporary Protected Status is already changing daily life for Haitians.** People are being called to ICE check-ins, fitted with ankle monitors or detained; expired work permits have forced people from their jobs and led others to stay home out of fear. [CNN](https://cnn.com/2026/08/09/us/haitians-tps-fear-arrests)

**Immigrants from South Sudan and Myanmar — also known as Burma — have seen TPS lapse as well** after federal judges in Boston and Chicago cleared the way to end TPS for them, effective August 7. The rulings end protection and work authorization for roughly 4,000 people from Myanmar and just over 230 from South Sudan. [The Hill](https://thehill.com/regulation/court-battles/6018220-dhs-ends-tps-myanmar-south-sudan/), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/8/us-courts-clear-way-for-deportations-of-south-sudan-myanmar-nationals), [AsAmNews](https://asamnews.com/2026/08/07/illinois-judge-denies-tps-extension-burma-residents/)

**Zomi Press, an outlet serving the Zomi diaspora from Myanmar, has a careful explainer:** TPS-based work permits are invalid as of the termination date, but technically, losing TPS does not by itself make someone deportable. [Zomi Press](https://zomipress.com/u-s-ends-tps-for-burma-and-south-sudan-what-the-august-7-termination-means/)

**For Venezuelans, most TPS protection has already quietly lapsed — October 2 covers only a narrow remaining slice of holders.** In Doral, Florida, lawyers and TPS holders are scrambling to sort out who the date still protects and what options remain. [Latin Times](https://www.latintimes.com/dorals-reckoning-inside-legal-scramble-before-venezuelas-tps-cutoff-october-2-598487)

**Somalia's TPS now hangs on a pending lower-court case — one of the last still standing.** Haiti, Syria, Yemen, Myanmar and South Sudan have all fallen within six weeks of a single Supreme Court decision. [Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/tps-legal-blows-full-list-communities-facing-deportation-12298749)

**Some 170,000 Salvadorans are approaching a September TPS expiration without clear official guidance — with an estimated 150,000 children affected.** In The New Yorker, Dara Lind puts the countdown inside what she calls the great delegalization: more than a million people watching the legal footholds that let them build lives in the United States get swept away. [Infobae](https://infobae.com/america/agencias/2026/08/10/unos-170000-salvadorenos-encaran-cuenta-regresiva-para-perder-el-tps-en-menos-de-un-mes), [The New Yorker](https://www.newyorker.com/news/the-lede/the-great-delegalization)

**Employers are pressing for replacement protections as Haitian workers lose authorization.** Health care and hospitality workplaces are absorbing sudden staffing losses; separately, 540-day automatic EAD extensions ended for certain TPS holders after August 5, and a federal court upheld a one-year cap on TPS work permits. [Miami Herald](https://www.miamiherald.com/news/business/article316803940.html)


## USCIS gains broader authority to move immigration filings online

**The new framework allows U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) to designate eligible forms for mandatory electronic filing.** Applicants will need to follow later agency notices to learn whether a specific request for an immigration benefit must be filed online. [USCIS](https://www.uscis.gov/newsroom/alerts/uscis-to-require-electronic-filing-of-forms-strengthening-national-security), [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/08/11/2026-16313/mandatory-electronic-filing-e-filing), [La Nación](https://lanacion.com.ar/estados-unidos/migraciones/ya-es-oficial-desde-hoy-el-uscis-ya-exige-la-presentacion-electronica-de-ciertos-formularios-para-nid11082026)

**The framework does not make every eligible form online-only at once.** Separate implementation steps will determine when paper filing ends for each form, a distinction that matters when applicants and their representatives prepare submissions. [Fragomen](https://www.fragomen.com/insights/united-states-uscis-announces-plan-for-limited-mandatory-electronic-filing.html)

**The shift lands on a system already processing enormous volumes with significant backlogs.** In the first half of fiscal year 2026, USCIS received 383,000 naturalization applications (N-400) with 649,000 pending; 230,000 family-based green card applications (I-485) with 580,000 pending; and 83,000 asylum applications (I-589) with 1.4 million pending. Work authorization applications (I-765) topped 1.2 million across all categories with nearly 2 million pending. [USCIS Immigration and Citizenship Data](https://www.uscis.gov/tools/reports-and-studies/research-and-statistics)


## Birthright citizenship challenge returns to court

**Immigrant-rights groups are asking a federal court to block a renewed executive effort to restrict birthright citizenship.** The dispute could determine whether children born in the United States are denied recognition of citizenship because of their parents' immigration circumstances. [ACLU](https://www.aclu.org/press-releases/legal-groups-that-secured-supreme-court-birthright-victory-move-to-protect-babies-from-trump-s-latest-attacks-on-citizenship), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/11/trump-faces-lawsuit-over-push-to-limit-birthright-citizenship)


## Around the system

- ICE’s expanded mandatory-detention policy is driving a surge of federal habeas challenges, straining judges and court staff as people contest detention without bond or try to fight removal without lawyers. [The American Bazaar](https://americanbazaaronline.com/2026/08/11/courts-overwhelmed-by-lawsuits-as-ice-detention-intensifies-486149)
- The State Department has revoked more than 175,000 visas under the Trump administration's immigration crackdown, citing birth tourism, alleged fraud, criminal activity, and some forms of online expression. A U.S. embassy in a North African country revoked more than 100 visas tied to birth tourism; reports from the border describe anxiety among Mexican visa holders whose travel histories may be reviewed for childbirth or earlier medical care in the United States. [El País](https://elpais.com/us/migracion/2026-08-11/la-administracion-trump-ha-revocado-mas-de-175000-visas-en-medio-de-su-ofensiva-migratoria.html)
- New York law requires local law-enforcement agencies to end their remaining 287(g) agreements with ICE and report how they are unwinding delegated immigration-enforcement work; the change does not restrict independent federal enforcement. [La Nación](https://lanacion.com.ar/estados-unidos/nueva-york/ice-en-nueva-york-las-12-agencias-policiales-que-deberan-terminar-sus-acuerdos-287g-antes-del-25-de-nid09082026)
- ICE procurement plans are drawing scrutiny from civil-rights advocates for contact-based electric-shock equipment, facial-identification searches and federal access to commercial data systems, with disputes centered on the use of force and the combination of government and commercial data to locate people. [CBS News](https://cbsnews.com/news/ice-officers-gloves-electric-shocks)
